Core Concept & Purpose

The ultimate goal here is to design classic, vintage-style t-shirts that look timeless. Crucially, these designs must be optimized to pop perfectly on either stark white or solid black t-shirt blanks. We want the artwork to speak for itself without relying on the shirt color to do the heavy lifting.

Illustration Style & Linework

Clean and Crisp: We are strictly omitting any grunge, distressed, or weathered texture effects. While we want a vintage look, the execution must be clean, flat vector-style artwork. The lines should be solid and crisp.

Hand-Drawn Aesthetic: The illustrations should feel organic and hand-illustrated, utilizing bold, distinct outlines similar to classic comic books or 1970s pop art.

Playful & Nostalgic Subjects: The imagery should evoke a sense of freedom and nostalgia. We are looking for:

  

Nature & Surf Culture: Scenic waterfalls , rolling waves , palm trees , and desert sunrises.

  

  

Flora & Fauna: Butterflies, caterpillars, and heavy use of stylized flowers like daisies and hibiscus. Whimsical elements like cats sitting among mushrooms and strawberries.

  

  

Retro Americana: Vintage pickup trucks parked in nature, classic concert tour posters, and whimsical 1930s/70s style "rubber hose" cartoon mascots (like the happy little house watering plants or the glass jar character).

  

Typography

Bold and Groovy: Typefaces should be a focal point, not an afterthought. We want chunky, retro serif and sans-serif fonts.

  

Warped & Flowing: Use typography that curves, waves, or warps to fit the framing of the illustration (like the circular framing of "A DAY DREAM AWAY" or the arched "HERE COMES" ).

  

Positive Messaging: The text should reinforce the feel-good visual themes. Incorporate positive affirmations and laid-back phrases like "SUMMER VIBES" , "ALWAYS GO WITH THE FLOW" , or "I am what I choose to become".

  

Color Palette

Muted Nostalgia: Lean into a 1970s and 1980s color story. Avoid neon or overly saturated modern digital colors.

Earthy & Pastel Tones: Use warm, sun-baked colors. Think mustard yellows, soft sage greens, faded denim blues, dusty rose pinks, and burnt oranges.

High Contrast for Blanks: Because these will live exclusively on black and white tees, ensure the color palettes have enough contrast to remain legible and vibrant against both extremes.
